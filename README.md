Quick Quiz

A simple quiz game that runs in your browser. Answer 10 questions, beat the timer and see your score at the end. Every game has different questions.

Play it here: https://aqsashaukat651-stack.github.io/Quiz-Game/

Features
10 random questions in every game
15 seconds for each question
Score and streak counter
Green for the right answer, red for the wrong one
Sound effects, with a button to turn them off
Light and dark themes with a switch button
Works on phones and computers
How to Play
Press Start quiz.
Choose one of the four answers before the timer ends.
Press Next question.
After 10 questions, see your score and press Play again.
